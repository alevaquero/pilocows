**Technical description:**  
Low-frequency (LF) RFID at 134.2 kHz, compliant with ISO 11784/11785 standards.

---

## For the database

The identification is an 11-digit sequential number. Tags are purchased in bags of 25 units each (button and flag), and the chip is located in the button.

### Registration of purchased tags
(This can be done manually or by scanning each tag; manual entry is likely best)

- Registration date  
- Tag number  

---

### Assignment of tag to cattle

- Registration date  
- Tag number  
- Breed (Angus, Shorthorn, Hereford, Holando Argentino, Brangus, Brahman, Limangus, etc.)  
- Category (bull, cow, heifer, steer, male calf, female calf, etc.)  
- Date of birth  
- Movement reason (birth, purchase)  

---

### Health records

#### Vaccination

- Procedure date  
- Tag number  
- Vaccine type (foot-and-mouth disease, brucellosis, anthrax, neonatal diarrhea, antiparasitic, respiratory diseases, fly control, etc.)  
- Detailed description  
- Supplier  
- Dose  

---

### Pregnancy status (cows)

- Procedure date  
- Tag number  
- Result (advanced pregnancy, mid pregnancy, early pregnancy, empty)  
- Action if empty (cull, last chance, remain in herd)  

---

### Blood testing for tuberculosis detection

- Date  
- Tag number  
- Result (positive / negative)  

---

### Weighing

- Date  
- Tag number  
- Weight  

---

### Removal from herd

- Date  
- Tag number  
- Movement (death, sale)
